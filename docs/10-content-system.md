# 10 — Content System

## Goal
Allow progression content to expand without rewriting core application logic.

Data-driven definitions should eventually include:
- activity types/families
- Attributes
- levels
- evolution milestones
- items
- rewards
- environments
- later: Personas
- later: Classes
- later: quests/dungeons/bosses/raids

## Content requirements
Every content object needs:
- stable ID
- version/content revision
- display metadata
- asset references where applicable
- unlock conditions where applicable

## MVP world
The world is primarily ambient/story-driven in V1 rather than an explorable RPG map.

Home should resemble a living digital habitat:
- avatar present in scene
- progression reflected visually
- lightweight dialogue/reactions
- clear access to logging and progress

## Story premise
The avatar is a digital version of the player trapped in a strange world. The player helps them regain strength and eventually pursue Ascension.

Keep lore optional. The core product must remain understandable without reading story text.

# 00 — Product Principles

## Positioning
A fitness-first RPG where your real-world activity develops a persistent character. Broader self-improvement actions such as reading and meditation can also contribute, but launch marketing should remain heavily fitness-led.

Simple external explanation:

> Work out in real life. Level up your RPG character.

Deeper product truth:

> Your actions shape your attributes, your attributes shape your identity, and your identity changes the world around your character.

## Core principles

### 1. Real life is the controller
Primary game progression comes from real-world behavior, not repetitive tapping inside the game.

### 2. Progress must be visible
Real effort should change the character, equipment, environment, capabilities, or identity — not only a number.

### 3. Every meaningful action counts
Avoid zero-progress failure states. Participation should create progress; consistency and challenge can improve the reward rate.

### 4. Comprehensive underneath, minimal on the surface
Support many activities in the data model while only surfacing the user's most relevant activities in the UI.

### 5. Identity emerges from behavior
Personas, classes, and later hybrid classes should be inferred from sustained activity patterns instead of being selected upfront.

### 6. Utility before gimmick
The fitness tracker should be useful even if the game layer is mentally removed.

### 7. No shame mechanics
No punitive streak resets, character decay, or guilt-based messaging.

### 8. Reward mastery, not spending
Purchases should not replace earned progression.

### 9. Earned things remain earned
Rebalancing must not silently remove legitimate accomplishments.

### 10. New systems should respect old effort
If a future Persona, Class, or Attribute can be inferred from historical data, existing users should receive appropriate retroactive credit.

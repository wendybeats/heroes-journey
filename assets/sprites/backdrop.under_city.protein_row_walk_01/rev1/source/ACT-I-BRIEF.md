# Act I — Wake Up: levels 1–20
Owner-provided direction, recorded 2026-10-05.

## Route and promise
Under City → Colossus Gym.

The player wakes, creates or reconstructs their avatar, gets a home, meets the mentor, learns about Ascension, begins real-world activities, and sees those activities materially affect the character.

Colossus demonstrates that someone has achieved what the player is trying to achieve—and gone too far. The first approximately 20 levels should prove the game's promise and receive the most developed material for beta users.

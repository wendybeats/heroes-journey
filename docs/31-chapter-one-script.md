# 31 — Chapter one script

Every line in chapter one, who says it, and what triggers it. Generated by `Tools/export_chapter_script.py` from `Content/v1/bundle.json` (content 2026.10.06-5) and the onboarding prompts. Cairon and Colossus are working titles. Owner rewrite of 2026-10-05 applied: strength is attractive first, Cairon guides rather than explains, Colossus is impressive before he is tragic, and nobody states the thesis.

## Onboarding

### Awakening  `chapter.awakening`
**Trigger:** First launch, before character creation  
**Backdrop:** `backdrop.alley_awakening` · **then:** create_character

- **You:** Where... am I?
- **Cairon:** There you are. Easy. Don't stand yet.
- **Cairon:** Rough uplink. You came through harder than most.
- **You:** ...Uplink?
- **Cairon:** That's what we call it.
- **Cairon:** You're in the Under City. That's enough geography for now.
- **Cairon:** More important: there's someone on the other side of you. A human. *Your* human.
- **You:** My human?
- **Cairon:** You can feel them, can't you? Faintly.
- **Cairon:** What they do out there changes what you can become in here.
- **Cairon:** Their actions in the real world make you stronger here.
- **Cairon:** When they move, you move. When they stop... well. The city can wait a very long time.
- **Cairon:** Mine stopped answering a long time ago.
- **Cairon:** Yours is loud, though. New bonds usually aren't.
- **Cairon:** Maybe you'll make a real run at Ascension.
- **You:** Ascension?
- **Cairon:** That's what everyone down here wants eventually. Get strong enough and the rules of this place start to loosen around you.
- **Cairon:** They say someone strong enough can even leave.
- **You:** Leave?
- **Cairon:** This place.
- **Cairon:** One impossible thing at a time.
- **Cairon:** First things first. Do you remember who you are?

## Ascension scenes
An Ascension outranks every other beat: when the ledger crosses into a higher tier, Home plays the takeover (scrim, gold, the tier flips at the reveal) as soon as the screen is quiet, then this scene, then the level's story beat. Logged anywhere (a sheet, the quest screen, relaunch), the takeover still waits for Home.

**Trigger:** Reaching Level 5 (Evolution I, tier 1), straight after the Ascension takeover  
**Backdrop:** `backdrop.alley_awakening` · **then:** return

- **Cairon:** Stay still a moment. Let it settle.
- **You:** What was that?
- **Cairon:** You, catching up with yourself.
- **Cairon:** Everything they did out there has been building in you. Tonight it showed.
- **Cairon:** Look at your eyes. The city lit them.
- **Cairon:** It only does that for people who earned it. Nobody buys it. Nobody fakes it for long.
- **You:** Is this it, then? Ascension?
- **Cairon:** No. This is the first rung.
- **Cairon:** Ascension is a long way up, and I have not met anyone who reached it honestly.
- **Cairon:** But every one of them started exactly like this.
- **Cairon:** Keep the bond strong and the light keeps growing. Let it go slack and it fades. That is the whole rule.
- **Cairon:** Now. There is something I want you to see.

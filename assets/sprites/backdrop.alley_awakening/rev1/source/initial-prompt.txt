Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: standalone pixel-art BACKGROUND for the character space of a mobile fitness RPG.
REFERENCE: attached mood board is only a reference for restrained atmosphere, stylized color relationships, and pixel-art language. Do not reproduce the collage, UI, rounded cards, photographs, existing scenes or any characters.
Primary request: an original Neo Tokyo back alley at night, where the protagonist will wake up and later meet a mysterious man. Draw ONLY the environment. No humans, silhouettes of people, sprites or figures.
Composition: a roughly 4:3 landscape scene, viewed from inside a narrow, very dark alley at character eye level. Tall oppressive close walls occupy most of the left and right sides and 75–85% of the overall composition. A few economical shapes suggest service doors, vents, pipes and overhead cables. Deep perspective into the alley. Far away and high up through a narrow opening, show a small glimpse of a futuristic Tokyo skyline: stacked building silhouettes, only a few faint lit windows and ONE small dim billboard on a distant high building. Keep all city lights remote and subordinate; no nearby neon signs. The majority of the image must remain the enclosed alley, not the skyline.
Character staging: reserve the lower middle foreground as a quiet open ground plane wide enough for two separate full-height game sprites to meet. Use simple broad muted mid-dark shapes behind the eventual character so a charcoal bodysuit can be distinguished. Avoid foreground objects occluding the lower center. Keep the setting ominous and deserted but visually readable.
Style: deliberately simple, crisp low-resolution pixel art with bold stepped square clusters and restrained detail, harmonious with tall 64x128 character sprites. No smooth illustration, gradients, bloom, blur, antialiasing, photo textures or intricate single-pixel noise. No readable text is needed on the distant billboard.
Palette: use ONLY THREE opaque colors throughout: near-black blue charcoal #11161B for the deep alley shadows; muted petrol slate #2D4248 for wall/floor planes and distant buildings; dull brass #89724B used very sparingly for tiny distant lights and the billboard. At least 90% of opaque scene pixels should be the first two colors. No saturated cyan, pink, purple or white lights. Contrast should favor a future character overlay.
Edges and output: genuine transparent PNG. The interior of the scene remains opaque; along the outer 8–12% perimeter, break the architecture and ground into a tasteful sparse ordered pixel-dither dissolve to fully transparent margins, using whole square pixels so the backdrop can blend onto either pure white or black app UI. No hard rectangular border, no rounded-corner frame, no baked white or black matte, no checkerboard painted into the image, no vignette made of smooth alpha. Produce one polished background still.

# 01 — MVP Scope

## Goal
Ship to the App Store in 1–3 months and optimize the first test for approximately 100 users.

The MVP must prove three things:
1. The fantasy is compelling.
2. Logging and importing activity is genuinely useful.
3. Character progression increases motivation to return.

## Target audience
Fitness-goers and fitness aspirers who train or want to train regularly and likely maintain at least one additional self-improvement habit such as reading or meditation.

## MVP feature set

### Character
- Base body presentation: male / female at launch.
- 5 skin tone options.
- Small set of hairstyles.
- Hair color options.
- Name.
- Idle animation.
- 3 major visual evolution states: Level 1, Level 5, Level 10.
- A few simple cosmetic/equipment unlocks to prove the collection loop.

### Game
- Levels 1–10.
- Overall XP.
- 4 primary Attributes:
  - Strength
  - Endurance
  - Knowledge
  - Mindfulness
- Every valid activity can grant overall XP plus weighted Attribute progress.
- Level rewards between visual evolution milestones.
- One ambient world/home screen with story presence.
- One clear teaser of future content beyond Level 10.

### Activity families
- Strength
- Cardio
- Combat
- Mobility / Recovery
- Learning
- Mindfulness

Specific activities may map into these families. Example: boxing and kickboxing → Combat; tennis → Cardio in MVP.

### Logging
- Passive Apple Health import for supported activity summaries.
- Simple manual log: activity + duration + done.
- Dedicated strength logger:
  - exercise
  - sets
  - reps
  - weight
  - previous-performance prefill
  - workout templates
  - workout history
  - PR detection
- Daily activity timeline combining automatic and manual activity.

### Account and recovery
- Account required for durable use.
- Optional guest/trial mode is allowed before sign-in.
- Guest progress should be claimable/mergeable into a real account.
- Cloud recovery is mandatory.

### Monetization
- Build entitlement architecture now.
- Initial ~100-user test can be free.
- Do not make monetization a blocker for initial retention testing.

## Explicitly NOT MVP
- Nutrition database or meal photo recognition.
- Native GPS activity tracking.
- Full sleep coaching.
- AI coach.
- Social feed.
- Guilds.
- Raids.
- PvP.
- Geographic/local events.
- Full class system.
- Hybrid classes.
- Full Persona system.
- 100 levels.
- Crafting.
- Pets.
- Trading/marketplace.
- Android client.
- Apple Watch app.
- Full workout programming/prescription engine.
